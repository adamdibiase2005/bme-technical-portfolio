## Company chosen - Intuitive Surgical

### Ticker
ISRG

### What the company does in plain English
Intuitive Surgical makes robotic surgery systems used by hospitals and surgeons. Its main product is the da Vinci surgical system. The company also sells instruments, accessories, and services connected to these systems.

### Why I chose it
I chose Intuitive Surgical because it connects biomedical engineering, robotic surgery, medical technology, and finance. Since I am a BME student, I wanted to analyze a company where the technical side and business side both matter.

### Why it connects to BME
It connects to biomedical engineering because robotic surgery involves medical devices, anatomy, safety, design, control systems, and healthcare technology.

### Why it connects to medtech finance
It connects to medtech finance because investors care about how many hospitals use the systems, how many procedures are performed, how much recurring revenue the company gets, and whether the company can keep growing profitably.

### What I want to learn from analyzing it
I want to learn how a medical technology company turns innovation into revenue, profit, cash flow, and long-term investor value.
