# Intuitive Surgical Company Overview

## What the company does
Intuitive Surgical is a medical technology company focused on robotic-assisted surgery. Its main platform is the da Vinci surgical system, which helps surgeons perform minimally invasive procedures.

## How it makes money
Intuitive Surgical generates revenue mainly from three sources: systems (da Vinci systems and Ion systems), instruments and accessories, and services (maintneance, repairs, tech support).

Systems revenue comes from selling or placing robotic surgical systems in hospitals. Instruments and accessories revenue comes from tools used during procedures, which makes it connected to procedure volume. Services revenue comes from maintaining and supporting the installed base of systems.

## Why it connects to biomedical engineering
The company connects to biomedical engineering because it combines robotics, medical devices, anatomy, surgery, software, control systems, hospital workflows, and patient care. It is a good example of how engineering becomes a real healthcare product used in clinical settings.

## What investors care about
Investors care about procedure volume, installed base growth, system placements, recurring instruments and accessories revenue, service revenue, margins, free cash flow, competition, hospital budgets, and regulatory risk.

## What I learned
This company is interesting because it is not just a one-time hardware sale. The installed base of da Vinci systems can support recurring revenue from instruments, accessories, and services. This makes Intuitive Surgical a useful company for learning how medtech businesses combine technology, hospital adoption, and financial performance.
