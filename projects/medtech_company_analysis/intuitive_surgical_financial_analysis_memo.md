# Intuitive Surgical Financial Analysis Memo

## Company Overview

Intuitive Surgical is a medtech company focused on robotic-assisted surgery. Its main platform is the da Vinci surgical system. The company is relevant to biomedical engineering because it combines robotics, medical devices, hospital technology, clinical workflows, and procedure adoption.

## Business Model Summary

Intuitive Surgical makes money through surgical systems, instruments and accessories, services, and operating leases. A major strength of the business model is that many revenue streams are recurring. Once hospitals use Intuitive systems, they continue buying instruments, accessories, and services as procedure volume grows.

## Financial Summary

From 2023 to 2025, Intuitive Surgical’s revenue increased from $7.1B to $10.1B. Net income increased from $1.8B to $2.9B, and free cash flow increased from $0.7B to $2.5B. This suggests that the company is growing while remaining highly profitable and cash-generative.

## Revenue Growth Analysis

Revenue grew 17.2% in 2024 and 20.5% in 2025. This suggests continued adoption of Intuitive Surgical’s robotic surgery platforms and growth in procedure volume. The acceleration in revenue growth is a positive sign because the company is already large but still growing at a strong rate.

## Margin Analysis

Gross margin stayed around the mid-60% range from 2023 to 2025. This suggests strong product economics. Operating margin improved from 28.1% in 2024 to 29.3% in 2025, suggesting operating leverage. Net margin also improved from 25.5% in 2023 to 28.6% in 2025, meaning more revenue is reaching the bottom line.

## Free Cash Flow Analysis

Free cash flow margin improved from 10.5% in 2023 to 24.7% in 2025. This is a strong improvement, but it should be interpreted carefully because capital expenditures as a percentage of revenue dropped sharply in 2025. The improvement suggests strong cash generation, but I should verify how sustainable the lower capex level is.

## Balance Sheet / Debt Context

Intuitive Surgical appears to have a strong balance sheet. I did not identify a traditional interest-bearing debt line, long-term debt line, borrowings line, or notes payable line in the annual report, so I treated total debt as 0 for this beginner analysis. However, I should verify the balance sheet notes before presenting this as a serious investment conclusion.

## Beginner Investor Takeaways

Intuitive Surgical appears to be a high-quality medtech company with strong revenue growth, high margins, improving profitability, and strong free cash flow generation. The company’s recurring revenue from instruments, accessories, services, and leases makes the business model especially attractive.

## Risks or Questions
- How sustainable is the lower 2025 capex level?
- How much future growth will come from procedure growth versus new system placements?
- Could new product launch costs, tariffs, or manufacturing expansion pressure margins?
- Should operating lease liabilities be considered when discussing debt?
